== WHAT STF IS ==
The Student Travel Fund (STF) gives financial support to Jacobs School of Engineering UNDERGRADUATE students to attend CONFERENCES and COMPETITIONS. It covers travel expenses like transportation and hotel costs, plus conference or registration fees. The fund is a gift from the proceeds of the Jacobs School Ring Ceremony. This year, TESC (Triton Engineering Student Council) manages the application process and reviews applications.
More info: https://tescatucsd.org/funding

== HOW TO APPLY ==
Apply with the Google Form: https://forms.gle/g6GC2MjhuTgNiB9k7
You must be signed in to your UCSD email account to open the form. All communication about your application is sent to the UCSD email you enter on the form.

The form asks for:
- Applicant info: student organization (if applying as part of one), first and last name, UCSD email, engineering department, number of students from your group attending (write 1 if you're the only one), whether you've applied for travel funds before, whether you're applying under a lab or research group with a faculty advisor, and the faculty advisor's and fund manager's names and emails (if applicable).
- Event info: name of the conference or competition, location, quarter, start date, end date, and anticipated cost.
- Impact: your purpose for attending (representing, competing, or a leadership role), how it supports your academic and professional development, how your participation benefits your research group, student organization, or the UCSD engineering community, and a 2-3 sentence summary of why the funding is important.

== REQUIRED FORM: STUDENT CERTIFICATION FOR BUSINESS TRAVEL ==
So the Dean's Office Financial Operations team can document your trip as business travel, fill out the "Student Certification for Business Travel" form and put a PUBLIC Google Drive link to it in your application.
Form PDF: https://tescatucsd.org/Funding/Student-Certification-for-Business.pdf
- Faculty signature section: if you are not applying as part of a lab or research group and don't have a faculty advisor, you can leave it blank.
- Chart string fields (Fund, Financial Unit, Function, Project, Task): if you are not applying under a faculty member or lab, you can leave them blank.
UCSD student travel policies: https://blink.ucsd.edu/travel/travel-policy-procedures/register-travelers/student-travel.html

== TIMELINE ==
1. Submit the application (Google Form above).
2. Review takes 3-4 weeks. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis (there are no fixed rounds). Apply at least 4 weeks before you travel: TESC cannot guarantee a response for travel happening sooner than that.
3. If you have not received an email within 3 weeks of applying, follow up by emailing contact@tescatucsd.org.
4. If your application is accepted, you'll get an approval email that includes a link to the Post-Travel Form.
5. After your trip, submit the mandatory Post-Travel Form WITHIN 2 WEEKS of travel, with ITEMIZED RECEIPTS and PROOF OF PAYMENT for every expense you want reimbursed. Requests received after that deadline are not processed and the award is forfeited. Reimbursement happens only after travel has occurred and documents are submitted.

== SUMMER TRAVEL ==
Applications are NOT reviewed during the summer. If you plan to travel over the summer, apply before the end of the previous academic year so your application can be processed.

== HOW MUCH / REIMBURSEMENT RULES ==
- STF is a REIMBURSEMENT program: you pay out of pocket first, then submit receipts to be paid back.
- Typical reimbursements range from $300 to $500, depending on your documented expenses and the funds available. This is a typical range, not a guaranteed amount.
- Only DOMESTIC travel (within the United States) can be funded. International travel cannot be funded.
- Student organizations: if your org applies for Student Travel Funds, it is NOT eligible for Matching Funds in the same academic year. Matching Funds: https://jacobsschool.ucsd.edu/idea/resources/matching-funds
